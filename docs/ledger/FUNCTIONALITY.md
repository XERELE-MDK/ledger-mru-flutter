# Ledger — how it works

[← All features](../README.md) · [Code walkthrough](CODE_WALKTHROUGH.md)

## What it does

Pick an account from the chips. For the month, the screen shows:
- the **opening balance** and the **closing balance**;
- every movement, with the **running balance** after it;
- total debits and credits.

Balances are shown on the account's **normal side**: debit for what you have and what you spend, credit for what you owe, what you earn and equity.
