# Empty book and demo — how it works

[← All features](../README.md) · [Code walkthrough](CODE_WALKTHROUGH.md)

## What it does

On first launch the app creates **three months of examples** in the phone's language.

- **Start with an empty book** (home card, or Settings) removes **only** the demo entries. Your entries, accounts and budgets stay.
- **Reset to demo data** (Settings) replaces everything, after a confirmation.

## While sync is on

- Removing the demo applies to every device, with no undo.
- Resetting to the demo is **refused**: it would spread to the other devices.
- Joining a folder already in use and merging leaves the demo out.
