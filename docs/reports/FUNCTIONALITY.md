# Reports — how it works

[← All features](../README.md) · [Code walkthrough](CODE_WALKTHROUGH.md)

| Report | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Trial balance** | every account on the debit or credit side at month end. Both totals must match. |
| **Monthly** | income and expenses over 6 months, average of non-empty months, comparison with the previous month |
| **Budgets** | spent / budget per category. Orange from 80 %, red above 100 % |

Tap a budget line to open the account and change the amount.
