# Accounts and budgets — how it works

[← All features](../README.md) · [Code walkthrough](CODE_WALKTHROUGH.md)

## What it does

Five account types: **asset** (what I have), **liability** (what I owe), **equity**, **income**, **expense**. An expense account can carry a **monthly budget**.

## Rules

- A name is required.
- An account used by an entry cannot be deleted.
- Changing the type of a used account reclassifies its whole history.
